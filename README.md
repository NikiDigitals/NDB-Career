## What This Is

NDB-CAREER is a structured Business Analyst career readiness programme running in parallel to the Life Enterprise System (LES) technical build and formal education (ACCA, OU BSc, UoL, Masters in AI).

It is not a LES module. It has its own document code, its own repository, and its own infrastructure.

The programme runs over 12 months and targets job-readiness as a Business Analyst in financial services by June 2027, with a 2–3 year target of Senior BA / Data Business Analyst, and a long-term trajectory of Finance Transformation Architect.
