*BCS BA Foundation · Data governance artefact · HBS case study #1 · PSM-I*

**BCS Business Analysis Foundation**

Strong credential for UK/NL financial services. Covers requirements engineering, stakeholder analysis, business change.

- Output: BCS BA Foundation certificate — Portfolio evidence

**PSM-I (Professional Scrum Master I)**

Agile/Scrum is a standard BA requirement. Pair with real LES artefact (sprint board, retrospective).
